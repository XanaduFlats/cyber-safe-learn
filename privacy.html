Privacy Policy

In short: CyberSafe Learn does not collect your data. Learning happens on your device. Nothing you type in the app is sent anywhere by us.

1. No account, no sign-up

There is no account, no email, and no password to use this app. We do not ask for your name, address, or any other personal information to learn.

2. No data uploaded by us

The app is a single static file. It has no server of its own, no tracking code, and no analytics from us. Your lessons, quiz answers, practice entries, and checklist are not sent to us or to anyone else by the app itself.

3. Progress is stored only on your device

Your progress (which lessons you finished, your badges, and your My Safety checklist) is saved in your browser's localStorage on your own device, under the key cyberlearn-progress-v1. It stays there. Clearing your browser data will clear it. We cannot see it, and we cannot restore it.

4. Practice uses pretend data only

The Practice section asks you to use pretend messages, links, and passwords. Never type your real password or real bank details into a practice box. Practice entries are checked only on your device and are never saved or sent.

5. Third-party ads (Google AdSense) — only when online

When this site is published, it may show ads from Google AdSense to help keep the app free. Ads appear only when your device is online, in one small slot on the Home screen, and never during a lesson. When you are offline, no ad is shown.

Google AdSense may use cookies to show ads. Those cookies are set by Google / third-party vendors, not by CyberSafe Learn. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or by using the opt-out pages for advertising networks.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your learning progress with advertisers.

6. No ads toggle

Use the “No ads” button at the top of the app to hide the ad slot. Schools and anyone who wants a clean screen can use this. The app still works fully offline with ads off.

7. No ads for children

This app is an education app for the general public. It does not show ads aimed at children, and it does not collect personal information from children.

8. Links to other sites

The app may mention real websites (for example, your bank's real website) in lessons. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. The date of the last update is shown below.
