Do not send passwords, bank details, or other private information by email. CyberSafe Learn will never ask you for those by email.

If you think you have been scammed, do not wait for an email reply. Tell your bank at once, change your passwords, and tell a trusted person. See the lesson “What to do if you have been scammed” in the app.
