Learn to stay safe online — offline, private, free.

About CyberSafe Learn

CyberSafe Learn is a free, offline learning companion that teaches cybersecurity to everyone, regardless of privilege.

It is for anyone, anywhere: people with a cheap phone, an old computer, or no internet at all. It is for first-time users, for families, for schools, and for anyone who has ever felt unsure about a strange message, a password, or a link.

No account is needed. No email is needed. No data leaves your device. Your progress is saved only on your device.

What it teaches

Six paths, in order, in short 2–5 minute lessons:

  1. Passwords and accounts
  2. Spotting scams and phishing
  3. Privacy and what you share
  4. Your phone and computer
  5. Money, jobs, and love scams
  6. Help others stay safe

Each lesson has a short story, one clear rule, something to try, and a tiny quiz. There is also a Practice section with safe simulations using pretend data only, and a My Safety checklist.

Free for everyone, schools included

The core app is free for everyone, including schools. Schools are not charged for safety. If the app shows ads, they appear only when the device is online, in one small, clearly labelled spot on the Home screen, and never during a lesson. There is a No ads toggle for anyone who wants a clean screen.

Private by design

The app is a single static file. It has no server, no tracking, and no uploads. Learning, practice, and progress all happen on your device.
